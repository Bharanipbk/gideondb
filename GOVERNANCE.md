# Project governance

GideonDB currently uses a maintainer-led governance model. Bharanikumar is the
initial maintainer and final decision maker for releases, security responses,
roadmap priorities, compatibility policy, and changes to governance.

Contributors earn trust through sustained, constructive participation and
sound technical judgment. The maintainer may grant review, triage, or commit
responsibilities and may later appoint additional maintainers. Repository
permissions are operational capabilities, not ownership of contributed work.

Technical decisions should normally be made through public issues and pull
requests using evidence, documented constraints, and rough consensus. The
maintainer resolves deadlocks and may require a design record for decisions
that affect public APIs, persistent formats, consensus, security boundaries, or
long-term maintenance cost.

Security reports, embargoed fixes, personal information, and credential matters
may be handled privately. Releases require maintainer approval and must satisfy
the project release gates. Governance changes are proposed by pull request and
record their rationale and effective date.
