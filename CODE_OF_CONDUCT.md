# Code of conduct

GideonDB contributors, maintainers, and community participants must help create
a respectful, harassment-free environment regardless of experience, identity,
background, disability, appearance, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or
technical preference.

Expected behavior includes being constructive and specific, assuming good faith
while addressing harmful impact, respecting boundaries and privacy, welcoming
new contributors, accepting correction, and focusing disagreement on ideas and
evidence. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination,
sexualized attention, insults, sustained disruption, doxxing, or retaliation.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and communications or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. They
will consider context, severity, history, safety, and proportionality, and will
protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Reports involving the
person who would normally receive them may be escalated through GitHub Support.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement principles.
